ようこそ

A neighborhood izakaya spirit, Florida made

O.E.C. has been rolling sushi and firing the hibachi for the Tampa Bay area from two kitchens — Japanese Express in St. Petersburg and Sushi 'n Hibachi in Largo. Everything is prepared when you order it: rice seasoned through the day, fish cut to order, vegetables from the morning delivery.

The menu runs deep — over 280 dishes — from a two-dollar egg roll to a sushi paradise platter for two. Come hungry, leave happy.
